Not a Prescriber Seat. A Share of the Clinic.

Dr Turton — you've built a reputation for treating the whole person: sleep, breath, nutrition, movement and the cause sitting underneath the symptom, with cannabis as one instrument rather than the product being sold. You've built it inside someone else's clinic. We're offering you the same medicine at national scale, as a part-owner and lead clinician of Golden Ratio: equity in the business, the clinical standard set by you, and your name on the front of it. Consults priced to be reachable rather than profitable — $99 initial, $30 re-script, and free outright through our referral door — a nurse-led team on the admin, sessional pay that never moves with script volume, and a regulatory framework already built to survive an audit on a Tuesday morning.

Why We Approached You

We Didn't Advertise. We Went Looking for a Doctor Who Practises Like This.

There are plenty of clinicians in Australia who will write a cannabis script. There are very few who treat it as one instrument inside a whole-person plan, and fewer still who'll spend a consultation on the patient's sleep, breathing, nutrition and history before reaching for a prescription pad. That is the practice we want at the centre of this clinic — not as one doctor's personal style, but as the house standard every clinician after you is hired into. That's why this page is addressed to you rather than to a job board, and why the offer is ownership rather than a roster spot.

🌿

Your Approach Is Already Our Clinical Model

Nutrition, breathwork, movement, sleep, meditation, temperature therapy, and real support around trauma, emotional health and addiction: that's the frame we want every Golden Ratio consultation to sit inside. We're not asking you to become a cannabis doctor. We're asking you to keep being the doctor you already are, in a clinic whose economics finally support it.

⏳

Time Is the Only Thing That Makes It Possible

You can't cover sleep architecture, breathing patterns and a trauma history in a seven-minute slot. Because we pay for hours rather than scripts, a 45-minute consultation costs the clinic exactly what a five-minute one does — so there is no financial reason, anywhere in this business, to hurry you. Consultation length becomes a clinical decision again.

🗺️

A National Book, Run From Perth

Golden Ratio is an Australia-wide telehealth clinic, not a state one. You'd practise from your own rooms in WA, keep your hours and your existing patients, and see eligible people in every state and territory we've cleared — which is all of them, because the state-by-state authorisation work is done before a patient is booked, never after. Your reach stops being a function of who can drive to Osborne Park.

⚕️

You'd Be Setting the Standard, Not Meeting One

As founding Authorised Prescriber you define what this clinic will and won't treat, how long a consult runs, what a proper follow-up looks like, and how much of the non-pharmacological work sits inside the care plan. Everything below is a proposal from us. The clinical half of it is yours to rewrite.

The honest version of the pitch: the medicinal cannabis sector has spent five years rewarding speed, and it is currently being audited for exactly that. The correction favours doctors who were never in that game. We would rather build a clinic around one of them and give them a real stake in it than hire fast now and apologise for it later — and on the evidence of how you practise, we think that doctor is you.

👤

The Clinical Face of the Business

Lead clinician, named and visible. The clinical voice across all four of our patient-facing front doors, in the patient education we publish, in GP referral outreach, and in the media work that comes with being the careful clinic in a sector short of them. The doctors who follow you are hired by you and mentored into your standard.

Equity On Top of Pay, Never Instead of It

The sessional rate stands at $220-250 per hour and is unaffected by whether you write a script. Ownership sits on top of that, not in place of it. And because your stake is in the whole business rather than in consult volume, the thing it actually rewards is a clinic with a durable reputation — which in this sector is decided by prescribing carefully, not quickly.

Why we'd hand over part of the business: we can build the software, the supply chain, the pharmacy relationship and the compliance machinery — all of that is well advanced. What none of us can manufacture is a clinician with a decade of credibility and a genuinely holistic way of treating people. That has to be someone's actual practice. If it's going to be the thing this clinic is known for, the person who brings it should own part of what it builds.
What We're Built On

Four Tenets, and a Group of Patients the Sector Keeps Missing

Every clinic says it is patient-first. Ours is organised around four words we actually use to settle decisions when they're difficult, and around a commitment to reach the people this industry has been worst at reaching.

🧠

Understanding

Know the whole person before treating the presenting complaint. History, sleep, stress, trauma, what's already been tried and what it cost them. Understanding is what a long consultation actually buys.

🤝

Respect

Patients arrive having been dismissed, lectured or moralised at — often for years, and often about cannabis specifically. They're treated here as adults with a say in their own care, and they're told the truth, including when the answer is no.

❤️

Love

The unfashionable one, and the one we mean most literally. Care given as if the person in front of you were family: unhurried, warm, and followed up because you actually want to know how they went.

🧘

Compassion

Someone deciding whether they can afford to ask for help is a clinical problem, not a marketing one. So the fee is held at $99 and the re-script at $30, the pre-screen costs nothing, and for anyone referred through our community door the consult is free. Nobody is turned away at the price.

🌎

Access for First Nations Patients

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Australians carry a heavier burden of chronic pain, trauma and sleep disorder than any other group in the country, and have the least access to a clinic like this one. Cost, distance and the very reasonable expectation of being judged are all in the way. This is exactly what jahman.clinic exists for: a referral-only front door where the initial consult is free, so a community health worker or a GP can send someone to us knowing the fee will never be the reason they don't turn up. Telehealth removes the distance, and how we train our nursing team is meant to remove the third barrier. We want to build this properly, with community-controlled health services rather than around them, and it's work we'd want our lead clinician driving.

🚪

Lowering the Doorstep

A lot of people who would benefit from this care never make the first appointment, because the clinical front door feels like the wrong one to knock on — or because they can't spare $99 that fortnight. So we meet patients in more than one tone: a calm clinical flagship, alongside warmer, community-minded entry points, including a referral-only door where the initial consult is free. Behind every one of them sits a single clinic, a single patient book and a single standard of care: yours.

Where your practice and our ethos meet: a clinic that means those four words has to give its doctor time, has to be relaxed about a consultation that ends without a prescription, and has to fund itself somewhere other than the consult fee. We've built the commercial model to do exactly that. What we haven't got is a clinician who already practises this way. That's the whole reason for this page.

Compensation & Alignment

Your Income Is Never Tied to a Script

The single most important thing a prescriber should understand about how this works: you are paid for your time, full stop. Whether you write a script or decide not to, your pay is identical. That isn't an accounting quirk; it's the deliberate foundation the whole clinic is built on.

⏱️

Sessional, $220-250/hr

You're engaged on a sessional or hourly basis at $220-250 per hour for clinical consultations, prescribing, TGA notifications, follow-ups and clinical-governance oversight. Hours are agreed up front and flex with you, not with the script count.

📊

A Cost Line, Not a Revenue Line

The clinic is funded by the wholesale margin on the medication it owns; your remuneration is a fixed cost the clinic pays, completely decoupled from what you prescribe. There is no bonus for volume, no per-script kicker, nothing that rewards writing more.

🛡️

Built for the AHPRA Era

Regulators are actively investigating clinics where prescribing and income move together. Here they can't. That alignment is your protection: every decision you make is defensible because you had nothing to gain from making it. You practise medicine; the clinic handles the commerce.

Why this matters for you specifically: the clinics getting into trouble are the ones that pay clinicians to keep the conveyor moving. We pay you to exercise judgement, including the judgement to say no. Your name on the prescription is only ever backed by your clinical reasoning, never by a commercial incentive, and that's a position you can comfortably defend to a board, a court, or your own conscience.

Clinical Autonomy

Your Prescribing, Your Call: We Handle the Paperwork

We're built around the TGA's Authorised Prescriber pathway as our primary route, with SAS-B for patients who fall outside AP categories, and every state and territory's own Schedule 8 layer mapped on top — including the Western Australian Medicines and Poisons Regulations, which we know sit differently to the eastern states. The regulatory machinery is ours to run; the clinical decisions are yours to make.

🏥

The AP Pathway, Managed For You

We prepare and manage your Authorised Prescriber application through the TGA, including the HREC or specialist-college endorsement. Once approved, you can prescribe to defined classes of patients without per-patient TGA approval.

Turnaround: typically 2-4 weeks for endorsement and TGA approval, with us doing the legwork.

📝

SAS-B, Pre-Drafted

For patients outside the AP class definition, we use SAS-B, a per-patient notification to the TGA. We maintain clinical-justification templates so the documentation is fast; you supply the reasoning and sign off, and the patient can be treated while the notification processes.

Process: notification submitted within 28 days of first prescription.

🌿

A Formulary You Help Shape

Because we own the pharmaceutical line, you'll have a stable, well-understood formulary to work from, and a real say in what we carry. You're never pushed toward a product; you prescribe what the evidence and the patient in front of you call for.

📋

A Scope You Set

We'll maintain a clear, documented list of the conditions we treat and the ones we won't, and you help define it. Patients who aren't appropriate for our care are declined or referred, not squeezed in. The boundaries are agreed in advance so no one is making a borderline call under pressure.

🏛️

Two Regulators, One Workflow

Commonwealth and state obligations are tracked against the same patient record rather than in two disconnected systems. The TGA side carries your AP approval and SAS-B notifications; the state side carries whichever S8 framework that patient sits under, WA's included. You see one completed checklist against one patient, and it's done before the consult reaches you.

🇦🇺

National From the First Consult

This is an Australia-wide clinic. Each state's authorisation and monitoring requirements are secured before the first patient is booked in that state, never after — NSW Health for unregistered S8, Queensland's Monitored Medicines framework, WA's thresholds, and the equivalents in VIC, SA, TAS, ACT and NT. You are never asked to prescribe into a jurisdiction we haven't already cleared, and you never carry the cross-border risk personally.

Aligned to RACGP prescribing guidelines and TGA Good Clinical Practice from the very first consult. Every consultation will be documented to a standard that would pass an audit tomorrow, not because we expect one, but because that's simply how careful medicine is recorded. You'll never be asked to cut a corner you'd have to explain later.
Medico-Legal Protection

We Protect Your Registration Like It's Ours

A prescriber's most valuable asset is their registration, and if you take equity in this business, its compliance record becomes your asset too. Both are the reason the framework below was designed before the first patient rather than after the first audit. It is the single area where we have been least willing to move fast.

Audit-Ready Documentation

Structured consult notes, documented clinical justifications for every patient, and templates that capture what an auditor would look for, so the record always backs the decision.

Minimum Consult Times & Load Caps

No three-minute scripts. We'll enforce minimum consultation times and maximum daily patient-load caps, so you're never pressured into rushed or volume-driven decisions.

Mandatory Follow-Up & Outcomes

Scheduled, nurse-coordinated follow-ups and outcome tracking are built into the model, demonstrating ongoing care rather than one-off prescribing.

Real-Time Monitoring, Every State

Every patient's history is checked in their state's real-time prescription monitoring system before a Schedule 8 cannabis product is prescribed, and the check is recorded in the note. For your WA patients that's ScriptCheckWA; interstate it's SafeScript, QScript and the ERRCD as applicable. Monitoring is never the gap in one of our files.

State S8 Authorisations, Handled

The most common compliance miss in this sector is forgetting the state layer on top of TGA approval. We hold it in every state we treat. In WA specifically, authorisation is required above the daily-THC thresholds (>40mg oral, >300mg inhaled) and for patients under 18, with prior substance use, or with a significant psychiatric history — those triggers are checked per patient inside the workflow, not remembered.

Clinical Governance & Audits

Quarterly clinical audits, a clear governance framework, and adverse-event processes you don't have to invent: the scaffolding that keeps a practice clean is designed in from the start, not bolted on later.

The AHPRA crackdown is an opportunity for careful doctors, not a threat. It clears the market of the bad actors that have damaged the field's reputation. By joining a clinic built from day one to do everything regulators are asking for (documented justifications, real follow-up, judgement-based prescribing, income decoupled from volume) you insulate your name from the very thing taking other clinics down.
The ducks, and where they're standing. Clinical governance, Best Practice templates and consult documentation designed against AHPRA's prescriber guidance and TGA Good Clinical Practice. State S8 authorisation coverage mapped jurisdiction by jurisdiction before we treat there. A partner pharmacy already holding its S8 permits, with a compliant affixed vault, per-brand drug registers, pharmacist verification of every TGA approval before handover, and mandatory counselling on dose, interactions and driving. E-prescriptions through HealthLink. Quarterly clinical audit and adverse-event processes written before they're needed. None of this is a plan we'd like to get to — it's the reason the clinic hasn't opened yet.
