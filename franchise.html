What Golden Ratio Does

  • Builds and deploys your clinic: patient website, booking, telehealth, backend, database and staff console, on your brand.
  • Integrates Zedmed practice management, eScripts, secure clinical messaging and My Health Record.
  • Connects your clinic to the pharmacy and warehouse so dispensing and shipping happen without you touching them.
  • Hands you a compliance framework built for TGA and AHPRA scrutiny, and keeps it current as the rules move.
  • Handles every IT problem for the life of the clinic. Outages, patches, security, upgrades. Ours, not yours.
  • Supplies the medicine. That is where we earn.

What You Do

  • Practise. You are the prescriber and the clinic is yours: the patients, the book, the brand, the profit.
  • Prescribe our formula as your first-line medicine where it is clinically right, and anything else where it is not. Clinical judgement stays with you.
  • Pay the running costs of your own tooling. The licences and subscriptions listed further down are yours, non-negotiable, and they are small.
  • Hold your own AHPRA registration, Authorised Prescriber status or SAS pathway, indemnity and business insurance.
  • Optionally, buy our setup and marketing help for a fee. Or do it yourself. Your call.
Why we would give away $200,000 of work. Because we are not a software company. We are a medicine company that built the software so its own clinic could run properly, and it turned out that the clinic was the hard part everyone else was missing. Every clinic on our platform prescribes our formulary, so every clinic we hand over is worth more to us running than the build cost us to make. You keep the clinic. We keep the medicine. Neither of us needs to take a cut of the other.

The Medicine

First-of-Its-Kind PGR-Free. Exclusive to Our Clinics.

Plant growth regulators are used across the industry to push yield and shelf appearance. Our formula is grown without them, and it is available only through Golden Ratio clinics. That exclusivity is the whole business model, so here is how it works and where the line is.

🌿

What It Is

A PGR-free medicinal cannabis formulary, first of its kind on the Australian market, produced to pharmaceutical standard and supplied through our dispensing pharmacy. Your patients cannot get it anywhere except a Golden Ratio clinic, and that is a reason for them to choose yours.

⚖

Where the Line Is

You prescribe our formula first-line where it is clinically appropriate. Where it is not, you prescribe what is, and the platform supports that. Nobody is paid per script, not you and not us, and no volume target exists anywhere in this arrangement. The regulator has been clear about what it will not tolerate, and neither will we.

💰

How We Get Paid

On the medicine, at the pharmacy. Not on your consults, not on your revenue, not on your profit. This is why there is no franchise fee: the build is our cost of putting the formulary in front of patients, and it pays for itself when your clinic is busy and well run. Our incentive is your clinic thriving.
