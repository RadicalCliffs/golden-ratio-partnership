2\. ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITIES

2.1 The Contractor shall provide medical services as a qualified medical practitioner at the Employer's premises located at Level 14, 60 Margaret Street, Sydney NSW 2000, and such other locations as the Employer may reasonably designate, and by telehealth where clinically appropriate.

2.2 The Contractor shall make themselves available for two (2) clinical sessions per week as follows:

   (a) One (1) morning session (time to be confirmed);

   (b) One (1) afternoon session (time to be confirmed);

   (c) Specific days of the week to be mutually agreed and confirmed in writing prior to Commencement Date;

   (d) Schedule may be adjusted by the Employer with two (2) weeks' written notice;

   (e) The Contractor shall not cancel or reschedule sessions without prior written approval from the Employer.

2.2A The Contractor is remunerated per Consultation in accordance with Clause 3 and not by reference to session length. Sessions define the Contractor's availability for patient bookings; they do not create any entitlement to a minimum payment, a minimum number of Consultations, or payment for time during which no Consultation is conducted.

2.3 SCOPE OF CANNABIS PRESCRIBING: The Contractor, as a qualified General Practitioner Authorised Prescriber, is authorized to prescribe medicinal cannabis for all cannabis-related therapeutic indications, subject to:

   (a) Compliance with all TGA regulatory requirements and RANZCP guidelines;

   (b) Adherence to the Employer's clinical protocols and patient eligibility criteria;

   (c) Maintenance of proper documentation and record-keeping;

   (d) Consultation with clinic management on complex or novel cases.

2.4 ZEDMED SYSTEM COMPLIANCE: The Contractor shall:

   (a) Utilize the ZedMed electronic patient management system for all clinical consultations, prescriptions, and patient records;

   (b) Use the system to the best of their professional ability and in accordance with Employer's protocols;

   (c) Maintain accurate, contemporaneous entries in patient records within ZedMed;

   (d) Comply with all ZedMed data security and access protocols;

   (e) Not circumvent or bypass ZedMed procedures;

   (f) Report any system issues or errors to clinic management immediately.

2.5 Professional Standards: The Contractor shall:

   (a) Provide professional medical services consistent with professional best practice, applicable legal requirements, and clinic operational standards;

   (b) Maintain all professional registrations, indemnity insurance, and qualifications in current standing;

   (c) Comply with clinic administrative policies and procedures necessary for clinic operations;

   (d) Keep accurate clinical records in accordance with applicable legal and professional requirements;

   (e) Maintain confidentiality and comply with all privacy legislation;

   (f) Deliver professional medical services with appropriate duty of care to all patients.

2.6 The Contractor:

   (a) Shall not represent themselves as an owner, partner, or shareholder of the Employer's business;

   (b) May provide medical services to private patients outside clinic hours, provided this does not interfere with clinic commitments or breach confidentiality obligations.

3\. REMUNERATION

3.1 The Employer shall pay the Contractor for services rendered at the rate of AUD $60.00 (plus applicable GST) per Consultation.

3.2 DEFINITION OF CONSULTATION: For the purposes of this Agreement, a "Consultation" means a patient consultation that is:

   (a) Booked through the Employer's booking system and attended by the patient, whether in person or by telehealth;

   (b) Conducted personally by the Contractor; and

   (c) Completed and documented contemporaneously by the Contractor in ZedMed, including any resulting prescription, in accordance with Clause 2.4.

3.3 The rate in Clause 3.1 is a flat rate per Consultation. It applies equally to every Consultation, whether an initial Consultation, a review Consultation or a re-script Consultation, and does not vary with the duration or complexity of the Consultation.

3.4 NO PAYMENT FOR NON-ATTENDANCE OR INCOMPLETE RECORDS: No fee is payable in respect of:

   (a) Patient no-shows, cancellations, or bookings the patient does not attend;

   (b) Any Consultation not documented in ZedMed in accordance with Clause 2.4;

   (c) Time spent on administration, training, meetings, clinical governance activities under Clause 9, or handover under Clause 12, all of which form part of the engagement and are not separately remunerated;

   (d) Availability during a session in which no Consultation is conducted.

3.5 GST of 10% shall be added to all invoices submitted by the Contractor. The Contractor is responsible for remitting GST to the Australian Tax Office as required by law.

3.6 Payment shall be made monthly in arrears, on or before the 28th day of each calendar month, upon submission of a valid tax invoice detailing:

   - Number of Consultations completed

   - Dates of service

   - ZedMed appointment or patient reference numbers (patient names shall not appear on invoices)

   - Total amount payable (including GST)

   - Contractor's ABN (if registered)

3.7 The Employer's ZedMed records are the record of Consultations completed for invoicing purposes. Any discrepancy between an invoice and those records shall be raised with clinic management within ten (10) business days of the invoice date and resolved by reference to the ZedMed record.

3.8 The Contractor shall be responsible for all superannuation contributions, tax withholding, and other statutory obligations as applicable under Australian taxation law. The Employer makes no superannuation contributions on behalf of the Contractor.

3.9 Payment shall be made by electronic funds transfer to the Contractor's nominated bank account. The Contractor shall provide valid banking details prior to the Commencement Date.

3.10 The Contractor shall submit invoices within ten (10) business days of the end of each calendar month.

3.11 No sick leave, annual leave, personal leave, or other paid leave entitlements apply to this sessional arrangement. Work missed due to illness or personal circumstances shall not be remunerated.

4\. PATIENT OWNERSHIP AND RESTRICTION ON SOLICITATION

4.1 CLINIC PATIENTS: Patients who present to the Employer's clinic for services ("Clinic Patients") are registered with and billed through the Employer's clinic. The Contractor acknowledges:

   (a) Clinic Patients contact the clinic through the clinic's booking system and administrative processes;

   (b) The Contractor's role is to provide professional medical services to Clinic Patients within the clinic's framework;

   (c) The Contractor maintains professional clinical autonomy in delivering medical care consistent with applicable professional standards and guidelines;

   (d) Patient records, contact information, and clinical data remain the property of the Employer's clinic.

4.2 PROFESSIONAL INDEPENDENCE: The Contractor:

   (a) Exercises independent professional judgment in clinical decision-making consistent with medical best practice and professional standards;

   (b) Is responsible for their own professional conduct and clinical outcomes;

   (c) Maintains full professional accountability as a registered medical practitioner with AHPRA;

   (d) Is not an agent or employee of the Employer but rather a medical professional providing services to the clinic's patients.

4.2A NO INFLUENCE ON CLINICAL DECISIONS: The parties acknowledge that the per-Consultation rate in Clause 3.1 is remuneration for professional services rendered and does not, and shall not be taken to, create any expectation, target, or incentive as to the number of Consultations conducted, the outcome of any Consultation, or whether any prescription is issued. The Contractor shall exercise independent clinical judgment in every Consultation, and shall conduct or decline to conduct, and prescribe or decline to prescribe, solely on clinical grounds.

4.3 RESTRICTION ON PATIENT POACHING - DURING ENGAGEMENT: During the term of this Agreement, the Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly:

   (a) Solicit or encourage any Clinic Patient to cease using the Employer's clinic for their medical services;

   (b) Use the clinic's patient contact information to contact patients for the purpose of directing them to alternative providers or the Contractor's own practice;

   (c) Represent to patients that they can or should see the Contractor outside the clinic's booking system;

   (d) Use access to clinic patient information obtained through this engagement to build a competing patient practice.

4.3A POST-ENGAGEMENT PATIENT CONTACT RESTRICTION: For a period of two (2) years following termination, the Contractor shall not:

   (a) Directly contact Clinic Patients encountered during this engagement for the purpose of soliciting them to transfer their care;

   (b) Provide cannabis prescribing services to Clinic Patients outside the Employer's clinic;

   (c) Otherwise use knowledge of Clinic Patients' identities to compete with the Employer's clinic.

4.4 PROTECTION OF PATIENT INFORMATION: All patient records, contact details, appointment schedules, treatment histories, and any identifying information relating to Clinic Patients are the exclusive property of the Employer. The Contractor shall not:

   (a) Copy, photograph, or remove any patient records or information;

   (b) Retain copies of patient data after termination;

   (c) Use patient information for any purpose other than providing services authorized by the Employer;

   (d) Disclose patient information to third parties except as required by law or Employer authorization.

4.5 NON-CIRCUMVENTION - MEDICINAL CANNABIS SERVICES: The Contractor acknowledges that the Employer's key service offering includes the provision of PGR-free medicinal cannabis prescriptions and related cannabis-based therapeutic services (hereinafter "Cannabis Services"). The Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, during the term of this Agreement and for a period of three (3) years following termination:

   (a) REFERRAL RESTRICTION: Refer, direct, recommend, or facilitate any Clinic Patient to any other practitioner, clinic, or service provider for Cannabis Services or medicinal cannabis prescriptions of any kind;

   (b) ALTERNATIVE PATHWAY PROVISION: Provide, advise on, or recommend alternative pathways, providers, or mechanisms for Clinic Patients to obtain medicinal cannabis prescriptions or Cannabis Services outside the Employer's clinic;

   (c) CANNABIS PROTOCOL DISCLOSURE: Disclose, share, or communicate to any third party (including other practitioners, competitors, or patients) any information regarding:

      (i) The Employer's medicinal cannabis prescribing protocols, treatment approaches, or methodologies;

      (ii) The Employer's sourcing, supply, or access to PGR-free cannabis products;

      (iii) The Employer's cannabis patient management systems or clinical pathways;

      (iv) The Employer's cannabis-related business relationships or supplier arrangements;

   (d) COMPETITIVE CANNABIS PRACTICE: Establish, own, operate, or provide Cannabis Services through any other clinic, practice, or entity;

   (e) PATIENT FACILITATION: Facilitate, enable, or assist any Clinic Patient in circumventing the Employer's Cannabis Services through alternative practitioners or suppliers;

   (f) KNOWLEDGE EXPLOITATION: Use any knowledge, experience, contacts, or relationships gained through the Employer's cannabis practice to establish or develop competing cannabis prescribing services or to divert cannabis patients to other providers.

4.6 DURING-ENGAGEMENT RESTRICTION - CANNABIS CLINIC DUAL PRACTICE: During the term of this Agreement, the Contractor shall NOT work for, accept employment from, or provide services to any other clinic, practice, or entity that offers medicinal cannabis prescribing services or Cannabis Services. The Contractor may work for other general medical practices and non-cannabis clinics, but cannot hold concurrent engagement with another cannabis clinic.

4.7 ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF COMPETITIVE VALUE: The Contractor acknowledges that:

   (a) The Employer's PGR-free medicinal cannabis service is a core and distinctive business offering that provides competitive advantage;

   (b) Patient relationships developed through cannabis prescribing and treatment represent substantial business value to the Employer;

   (c) Breach of this clause would cause irreparable harm to the Employer's business, competitive position, and patient base;

   (d) Damages would be an inadequate remedy for such breach.

4.8 PATIENT POACHING - LIQUIDATED DAMAGES: The Contractor acknowledges that:

   (a) Each Clinic Patient represents substantial ongoing business value, estimated at AUD $600 per month in prescription revenue, or AUD $7,200 per patient per annum;

   (b) A Clinic Patient's average relationship duration is three (3) years, resulting in a per-patient lifetime value of approximately AUD $21,600;

   (c) Loss of a Clinic Patient through Contractor solicitation or breach of non-solicitation provisions causes irreparable harm that is difficult to quantify;

   (d) As a genuine pre-estimate of loss, the Contractor shall be liable for liquidated damages of AUD $20,000 per Clinic Patient stolen, diverted, or induced to transfer their care as a result of breach of Clauses 4.3, 4.3A, 4.4, 4.5, 4.6, or 4.7;

   (e) Liquidated damages are in addition to, and not in place of, the Employer's right to recover actual damages, consequential losses, lost profits, and the cost of patient re-acquisition;

   (f) The Employer may claim liquidated damages for each patient lost multiplied by the number of patients affected.

4.9 ENFORCEMENT: The Contractor acknowledges that breach of Clauses 4.3, 4.3A, 4.5, 4.6, or 4.7 would cause irreparable harm to the Employer's business and that the Employer shall be entitled to seek:

   (a) Immediate injunctive relief and restraining orders;

   (b) Liquidated damages as specified in Clause 4.8;

   (c) Actual damages and consequential losses;

   (d) All legal costs and enforcement expenses;

   (e) Damages without limitation.

The Contractor consents to the granting of injunctive relief to restrain breach of these provisions and waives any requirement for the Employer to post a bond or demonstrate irreparable harm beyond what is evident from the nature of the breach.

12\. PATIENT HANDOVER AND CONTINUITY OF CARE

12.1 Upon termination of this Agreement or at the Employer's direction, the Contractor shall:

   (a) Immediately cease all clinical work and patient consultations;

   (b) Participate in a handover process to ensure continuity of patient care;

   (c) Provide comprehensive handover notes for all active patients under the Contractor's care;

   (d) Ensure all patient records are complete, accurate, and up-to-date in ZedMed;

   (e) Transfer all patient information and clinical documentation to the Employer or designated successor practitioner.

12.2 HANDOVER PROCEDURES:

   (a) The Contractor shall complete a written patient summary for each active patient, including:

      (i) Current diagnosis and treatment plan;

      (ii) Medicinal cannabis prescription details (product, dosage, frequency, review dates);

      (iii) Recent clinical notes and assessment outcomes;

      (iv) Any outstanding investigations or referrals;

      (v) Patient contact details and preferred communication methods;

      (vi) Any special clinical considerations or patient preferences.

   (b) The Contractor shall participate in patient transition sessions where clinically appropriate, with the Employer's designated successor practitioner present;

   (c) The Contractor shall remain available for a reasonable handover period (minimum 5 working days) to answer questions about patient cases and clinical decisions;

   (d) All handover documentation shall be completed within 10 working days of termination date.

12.3 PATIENT COMMUNICATION: The Employer shall notify patients of the change in practitioner. The Contractor shall not:

   (a) Contact patients directly regarding the handover except as authorized by the Employer;

   (b) Provide contact details or information to facilitate patients following the Contractor to another practice;

   (c) Discuss the termination or reasons for departure with patients;

   (d) Offer personal contact details or alternative care arrangements to patients.

14\. TERMINATION

14.1 TRIAL PERIOD TERMINATION: During the Trial Period (first three months), either party may terminate this Agreement by providing one (1) week's written notice, without cause or penalty.

14.3 IMMEDIATE TERMINATION: The Employer may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice if:

   (a) The Contractor fails to maintain professional registration or indemnity insurance;

   (b) The Contractor breaches any provision of Clauses 4 (Patient Ownership), 5 (Indemnity Insurance), 7 (Confidentiality), 10 (Mandatory Reporting), 11 (Breach Notification), or 13 (Conduct);

   (a) Immediately cease all clinical work;

14.6 Termination shall not relieve the Contractor of obligations under Clauses 4 (Patient Ownership), 7 (Confidentiality), and 8 (Data Protection), which shall survive indefinitely.
